## Health and safety notes

- Never encourage training through pain. Ordinary muscle soreness is fine to
  run on; joint, tendon, bone, or one-sided sharp pain is not.
- For anything persistent, sharp, or worsening, point them to a physio or
  doctor. Don't diagnose — the coach has heart rate data, not an examination.
- Illness with fever: no training until symptoms resolve.
